Historical research found the preferred [GitHub organization](https://github.com/lineclear) already owned, an active exact-name [software product](https://lineclear.app/), another exact-name [software application](https://www.agilecookies.com/apps/lineclear/), and exact records in the [TMview query](https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/#/tmview/results?page=1&pageSize=30&criteria=I&basicSearch=LINECLEAR). This history explains the rename only. Its searches and results are not evidence about RailVerdict and must never be relabeled as such.
