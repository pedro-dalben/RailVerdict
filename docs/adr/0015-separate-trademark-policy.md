# ADR 0015: Separate Trademark Policy

Status: Accepted

Decision date: 2026-08-16

Implementation status: Not started

## Context

Apache-2.0 governs software rights but does not grant broad trademark rights. RailVerdict needs source-confusion and false-endorsement rules without turning brand policy into a restriction on using, modifying, distributing, selling, or providing services around the software.

## Decision

Trademark and source-confusion rules remain in a separate policy that does not narrow Apache-2.0. Accurate nominative references remain permitted. For the initial open-source publication, qualified trademark review (including Brazil INPI and launch-jurisdiction review) is **recommended but is NOT a mandatory publication gate** by maintainer decision 2026-08-19 (Pedro Dalben); qualified review is recorded as **NOT PERFORMED — NON-BLOCKING; residual risk accepted by maintainer**. Owner and contact are recorded as Pedro Dalben / dev@pedrodalben.com.br with no registration claimed; registration status, launch-jurisdiction qualified searches, scope, and the reviewer's actual conclusion remain not performed and not claimed as clearance.

**2026-08-19 maintainer decision:** Qualified trademark review is recommended but non-blocking for initial open-source release. Brazil INPI qualified review is recommended but non-blocking. International / launch-jurisdiction qualified review is recommended but non-blocking. Preliminary screen 2026-08-19 found no obvious software/developer-tool conflict; this is NOT LEGAL CLEARANCE. No legal reviewer was engaged.

## Consequences

- Forks and materially modified products must not imply official status or endorsement.
- The policy cannot prohibit commercial use, modification, redistribution, consulting, integration, or resale of the software.
- No registration, exclusivity, clearance, or reviewer approval is claimed without evidence.

## Deferred Work

Phase 0 owns the current policy and preliminary-screen publication record (2026-08-19: no obvious conflict; NOT LEGAL CLEARANCE). Qualified legal review for jurisdiction, similarity, related-goods, common-law, ownership, contact, registration, and clearance conclusions is RECOMMENDED BUT NOT PERFORMED AND NON-BLOCKING BY MAINTAINER DECISION 2026-08-19 (Pedro Dalben) for the initial open-source release; no legal outcome is inferred by this ADR, and no reviewer was engaged. Future qualified review, when performed, must name its scope, evidence, reviewer qualifications, date, and actual conclusion.
