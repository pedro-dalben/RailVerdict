# ADR 0015: Separate Trademark Policy

Status: Accepted

Decision date: 2026-08-16

Implementation status: Not started

## Context

Apache-2.0 governs software rights but does not grant broad trademark rights. RailVerdict needs source-confusion and false-endorsement rules without turning brand policy into a restriction on using, modifying, distributing, selling, or providing services around the software.

## Decision

Trademark and source-confusion rules remain in a separate policy that does not narrow Apache-2.0. Accurate nominative references remain permitted. Publication remains blocked pending qualified review, and the owner and contact remain unresolved alongside registration status, launch-jurisdiction searches, scope, and the reviewer's actual conclusion.

## Consequences

- Forks and materially modified products must not imply official status or endorsement.
- The policy cannot prohibit commercial use, modification, redistribution, consulting, integration, or resale of the software.
- No registration, exclusivity, clearance, or reviewer approval is claimed without evidence.

## Deferred Work

Phase 0 owns the current policy and unresolved publication record. Pre-publication qualified legal review owns the jurisdiction, similarity, related-goods, common-law, ownership, contact, registration, and clearance conclusions; no legal outcome is inferred by this ADR.
